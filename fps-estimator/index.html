Why is calculating FPS important?

  • Smooth experience: 60 FPS on PC, 30-60 on mobile is the quality standard
  • Competitive edge: Games with better performance attract more users
  • Proactive optimization: Identify bottlenecks before they affect users
  • Feature planning: Know how many effects/polygons you can add without losing performance

📚 FPS Optimization Guide

  • Reduce Draw Calls: Use material batching, texture atlases, GPU instancing
  • Optimize Geometry: Implement LOD (Level of Detail), culling, mesh simplification
  • Efficient Shaders: Use forward rendering for few lights, deferred for many
  • Post-processing: Half-resolution or quarter-resolution for heavy effects
  • Dynamic Shadows: Limit distance, reduce cascades, use shadow atlasing
  • Profiling: Use Unity Profiler (CPU/GPU) to find real bottlenecks
  • Targeting: Different configs for different devices/platforms
